How to Order

  1. Browse our product catalogue
  2. Select items you're interested in
  3. Click "Check Price & Stock" to view our WhatsApp Channel
  4. Message us on WhatsApp quoting the piece number
  5. Confirm your order and delivery details
  6. Receive your tracking code

Delivery Information

We deliver throughout Ghana. Delivery fees and timing depend on your location. Contact us on WhatsApp for specific delivery information for your area.

Ask Our Concierge

Hello! I'm here to help. Ask me anything about our products, ordering, or services. For detailed enquiries, I can connect you to WhatsApp.

Browse our product catalogue, click on any item to view details, and check current prices and stock on our official WhatsApp Channel. When you are ready, message our team on WhatsApp with the piece number and we will guide you through the order.

We believe in personalized service. Prices may vary based on availability, promotions, and special requests. Current prices are published on our official WhatsApp Channel.

Once your order is confirmed, you'll receive a unique tracking code. Visit our Track Order page and enter your code to see the current status of your order.

We accept mobile money (MTN, Vodafone, AirtelTigo), bank transfers, and cash on delivery within Accra. Payment details are provided during the ordering process.

Currently, we primarily serve customers within Ghana. For international shipping requests, please contact us on WhatsApp to discuss options.
